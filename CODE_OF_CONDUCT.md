# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want leadgen to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Assume good faith; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Using the project to promote spam, scraping of protected data, or resale of
  personal data (see `docs/RESPONSIBLE_USE.md`).

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, and
any official communication channels) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may block repeat or serious offenders. To report a concern,
contact the maintainer via the repository's contact channel or a private message.
Reports are handled discreetly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
